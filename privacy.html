Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

KRYPTN ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the KRYPTN Creator Suite.

1. Data We Collect

We only collect data necessary to provide our services. This includes:

  • TikTok Account Information: Through the TikTok Login Kit, we receive your public profile information and authorized scopes to enable video uploading.
  • Media Assets: We process the video files you choose to upload to TikTok. These are processed locally or temporarily and are not stored on our servers longer than necessary for the upload.

2. How We Use Data

Your data is used solely to facilitate the scheduling and posting of content to your TikTok account as directed by you. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information. Since KRYPTN operates as a local developer toolkit, your credentials (like Client Secrets) should be managed securely on your local environment.

4. Third-Party Services

Our tool interacts with TikTok via official APIs. We encourage you to review the TikTok Privacy Policy.

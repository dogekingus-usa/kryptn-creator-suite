Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 27, 2026

1. Introduction

The KRYPTN Creator Suite ("we," "our," or "the Application") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our application to manage your social media content through the TikTok and YouTube APIs.

2. Information We Collect

Our application operates primarily in a local environment. We do not maintain external servers to store your personal data. The types of information accessed include:

  • Authentication Data: We use OAuth 2.0 to securely authenticate your TikTok and YouTube accounts. Your login credentials are never stored by the Application.
  • Profile Information: We may access your display name and profile picture to personalize your local dashboard.
  • Content Metrics: We retrieve metadata regarding your videos (view counts, likes, etc.) to provide local analytics.

3. How We Use Information

Purpose Description
Analytics Displaying performance metrics to help optimize your content strategy.
Workflow Management Facilitating the scheduling and uploading of media files to authorized channels.
Account Identification Ensuring the tool is interacting with the correct authorized user profile.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell your data. We do not share your personal information with any third-party companies or advertisers. All data processing occurs locally on your machine.

5. Data Retention

Data retrieved from APIs is stored temporarily in your local session. You can revoke access at any time through your Google or TikTok security settings.

6. Contact Information

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the official contact information provided in the Developer Portal.

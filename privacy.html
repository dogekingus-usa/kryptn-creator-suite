Privacy Protocol

KRYPTN Creator Suite // Deployment Secure

1. Data Architecture

KRYPTN operates as a local infrastructure tool. We prioritize the "Principle of Least Privilege" regarding your data:

  • API Credentials: Your TikTok Client Key and Secret are stored locally in your environment. We never transmit or store these on our own servers.
  • Media Assets: Video files are processed within your production pipeline. We do not maintain copies of your creative assets after the deployment is complete.

2. Platform Integration

Our suite utilizes the official TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API. By using KRYPTN, you authorize the suite to:

  • Access your public profile information for authentication.
  • Upload and manage video content as directed by your local commands.

3. Third-Party Governance

Data transmission occurs directly between your local instance and TikTok's official servers. We recommend reviewing the TikTok Privacy Policy for information on how they handle data post-deployment.

4. Security Standards

We implement strict encryption protocols for all data in transit. As a developer-focused toolkit, we ensure that session tokens and OAuth2 data are handled according to industry-standard security practices.
